# Priorities

Ordered by what matters **now**. Revisit each session.

1. **UI concept quality (Phase 0).** Everything hinges on this. The bar is
   "commercial-product polish, sci-fi / hi-tech". If the UI is not convincing, the
   project does not proceed.
2. **Constraints that shape the UI**: 480×320 landscape, capacitive touch (finger
   targets, not stylus), readable at a glance from across a room.
3. **Deferred until after the gate**: HA connection method, firmware, relay wiring.

## Not now (explicitly parked)

- On-device scheduling — out of scope forever.
- HA connection decision — [ADR-0005](adr/0005-ha-connection-method.md), decide later;
  does not block Phase 0.
